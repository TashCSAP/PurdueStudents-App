Christian Students at Purdue has been helping students grow in Christ for over 30 years. We are a student group composed of believers in Christ from various backgrounds. We stand absolutely for the faith that is common to all Christians.

We love the Lord Jesus Christ, and endeavor to give Him "the first place in all things" (Col. 1:18). We have the utmost respect for His Word, and meet simply as Christians without any denominational affiliation because we treasure the testimony of the oneness of the Body of Christ.

Our Mission

Our goal is to provide Purdue University students with a genuine community of believers with whom they can pursue Christ (Phil. 3:8, 2 Tim. 2:22), to solidify them in the truth of God's word, and to equip them for their futures in God's Purpose.

The Bible

The Bible is the complete divine revelation inspired word by word by God through the Holy Spirit.

— 2 Pet. 1:21, 2 Tim. 3:16

God

God is uniquely one, yet triune — the Father, the Son, and the Spirit.

— 1 Tim. 2:5a, Matt. 28:19

Jesus Christ

The Son of God, even God Himself, was incarnated to be a man by the name of Jesus Christ.

— John 1:1, John 1:14

Redemption

Jesus died on the cross for our sins, shedding His blood for our redemption.

— 1 Pet. 2:24, Eph. 1:7a

Resurrection

Jesus resurrected from among the dead on the third day.

— 1 Cor. 15:4

Salvation Through Faith

Whenever any person repents to God and believes in the Lord Jesus Christ, he is regenerated (born again) and becomes a living member of the one Body of Christ.

— Acts 20:21, John 3:3, Eph. 1:22-23, Rom. 12:5

The Lord is Coming Again a Second Time

Christ is coming again to receive His believers to Himself.

— 1 Thes. 2:19
